3. Mobile "Quick-Update" Form
Updating a Google Sheet on a phone during hospital rounds or on the bus is a nightmare.

The Idea: Create a Google Form linked to this sheet.

The Workflow: 1. The Form has two fields: Topic Name (Dropdown) and Self-Rating (0-5). 2. When you submit the form, a script automatically finds that topic in your S8 sheet and updates the date and mastery for you.

Why: You can finish a review and update your tracker in 3 taps on your phone without ever opening the actual spreadsheet.
